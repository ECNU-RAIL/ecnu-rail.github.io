About

RAIL Lab is a research team at East China Normal University (ECNU) focusing on reasoning, agentic and alignment laboratory.

Our mission is to build intelligent systems that move beyond static pattern matching: systems that reason reliably, act autonomously in digital and physical environments, and remain aligned with human goals.

Our research centers on the three pillars of RAIL—Reasoning, Agentic AI, and Alignment—across large language models, multimodal foundation models, and agents. By bringing together reasoning, perception, planning, interaction, and learning from feedback, we aim to create AI systems that can solve complex, long-horizon problems and continually improve through experience.
